Common Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does submission-only format work? What happens if nobody submits?

In submission-only format, the only way to win is by submission — no judges, no points, no decisions. If a regulation period ends without a submission, the match goes into overtime. In overtime, each competitor starts in a compromised position (back take, mounted triangle, etc.) and must either escape or submit. If neither athlete submits in overtime rounds, the match is declared a draw. We prefer a draw over a bad decision every time.

Can I compete if I've never entered a tournament before?

Absolutely. Finishers Circle is open to all experience levels. We have divisions specifically designed for beginners and white belts. Our No-Gi Beginner division is perfect for athletes with under one year of training. We encourage first-time competitors — the only way to grow is to compete. Come test yourself in a safe, professionally run environment.

How does athlete pay work? Who is eligible?

Finishers Circle is the first tournament series in Central America to offer direct athlete compensation. Prize money is distributed to division winners and — in featured matches — to both submission bonuses and event winners. The total prize pool is announced prior to each event and scales with registration numbers. Details for each tournament's prize structure are shared on our Instagram and emailed to registered competitors.

What is the refund and cancellation policy?

We understand that life happens. Registration fees are fully refundable up to 14 days before the event date. Within 14 days but before 7 days out, we offer a 50% refund or a transfer credit to a future Finishers Circle event. Within the final 7 days, fees are non-refundable but credits may be issued at the organizer's discretion for medical emergencies with documentation. Contact us directly to discuss your situation — we work with competitors whenever possible.
