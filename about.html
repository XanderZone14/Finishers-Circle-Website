ABOUT & MISSION

The Story Behind the Circle

Our Story

HOW IT ALL BEGAN

Finishers Circle was born from a simple, uncompromising belief: the sport of submission grappling deserves better in Central America. For too long, athletes in Belize and across the region had nowhere to compete at a level that matched their dedication, their sacrifice, and their skill.

We saw fighters training daily — sometimes twice a day — under brutal heat, with limited equipment, against all odds, yet never getting the recognition or the real competitive platform their work demanded. The existing tournament landscape was built around points systems that rewarded stalling, passive play, and politics over pure fighting ability. That wasn't good enough.

So we built something different. Something honest. Something that puts the athlete first.

We are the first platform in Central America to pay our athletes for their performance. When you finish your opponent, you earn — because that's what the sport deserves. A rear naked choke lands different when there's real money behind it. A triangle choke means more when you know it takes food off someone else's table and puts it on yours. Finishers Circle is where grappling becomes a career, not just a hobby.

What We Stand For

OUR MISSION

Three pillars drive everything we do. No exceptions. No compromise.

Submission Excellence

Every event is 100% submission only. No points. No decisions. If you want to win, you finish. We hold this standard at every bracket, every division, every round — no exceptions. The format forces athletes to attack, evolve, and perform at their absolute peak.

Athlete Compensation

We pioneered athlete pay in Central America. Winners earn real money for real submissions. We believe fighters who dedicate their lives to the craft deserve financial recognition. The prize pool grows with every tournament as the community grows with us.

Community Growth

Finishers Circle exists to grow grappling culture in Belize and Central America. We work with local gyms, coaches, and athletes to build a sustainable ecosystem — one where the next generation has a stage, a standard, and a path forward in the sport they love.

The Philosophy

WHY SUBMISSION ONLY?

Points-based grappling rewards playing it safe. A fighter can win by pulling guard and stalling for twelve minutes. They can win by securing a single takedown and defending for the rest of the match. They can win without ever trying to finish — without ever truly putting themselves on the line.

That's not fighting. That's gaming a system.

Submission-only rules strip all of that away. There is nowhere to hide. You must attack. You must take risks. You must commit to the finish or go home with nothing. This format rewards technique, creativity, and genuine grappling ability — not stall tactics and judge manipulation.

It also makes for better competition to watch. Fans understand a rear naked choke. They understand a heel hook, an armbar, a guillotine. There's no scorecard to argue about, no controversial decision. The mat tells the truth every single time.

Submission-only is also the purest link to grappling's origins — Judo, Wrestling, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Catch Wrestling. The goal has always been to control and submit your opponent. We honor that tradition while building something new for Belize.

From the Founder

FOUNDING VISION

“

I started training BJJ in Belize because I fell in love with the problem-solving nature of grappling — the way every roll is a conversation in real time, a chess match fought with your body. But as I got deeper into the sport, I realized there was nowhere here to truly compete. Nowhere to test yourself on a real stage. Nowhere to prove that the work you were putting in meant something beyond the walls of your gym.

So I decided to build that place. Not just a tournament series — a platform. Something that takes the athletes of Belize and Central America seriously. Something that says: your dedication is worth compensating. Your submissions are worth celebrating. Your career in this sport is worth pursuing.

Finishers Circle is still young. We're building it event by event, athlete by athlete, submission by submission. But the vision is clear: we want to put Belize on the grappling map — not just in Central America, but in the world. The talent is here. The heart is here. Now the platform is here too.

Come find out if you belong in the Circle.

— Founder, Finishers Circle
