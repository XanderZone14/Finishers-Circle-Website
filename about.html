ABOUT & MISSION

The Story Behind the Circle

Our Story

HOW IT ALL BEGAN

It started with what most people called a crazy idea. Not because there was nowhere to compete — athletes in Belize have always found a way. But the experience? The atmosphere? The feeling of stepping onto a mat and being treated like the legitimate, celebrated athlete you are? That part was missing.

We looked at how tournaments were being run locally and regionally and asked a simple question: why does competing feel like a formality rather than an event? Why do our athletes walk in, fight, and walk out feeling like they just ran an errand?

We wanted to curate something different — a production. An experience where Belize City's own athletes could step onto that mat and feel like the local superstars they already are. Real presentation. Real stakes. A crowd that knows their names.

So we threw out the traditional format entirely. A 16-man absolute bracket. A round robin where everyone fights everyone. Tag team duels. Every event designed as an experience first, a competition second — because when the atmosphere is right, the fighting is better, the crowd is louder, and the athletes rise to meet the moment.

That's how Finishers Circle was born. Not from necessity — from ambition.

What We Stand For

OUR MISSION

Three pillars drive everything we do. No exceptions. No compromise.

Submission Excellence

Every event is 100% submission only. No points. No decisions. If you want to win, you finish. We hold this standard at every bracket, every division, every round — no exceptions. The format forces athletes to attack, evolve, and perform at their absolute peak.

Athlete Compensation

We pioneered athlete pay in Central America. Winners earn real money for real submissions. We believe fighters who dedicate their lives to the craft deserve financial recognition. The prize pool grows with every tournament as the community grows with us.

Community Growth

Finishers Circle exists to grow grappling culture in Belize and Central America. We work with local gyms, coaches, and athletes to build a sustainable ecosystem — one where the next generation has a stage, a standard, and a path forward in the sport they love.

The Philosophy

WHY SUBMISSION ONLY?

Points-based grappling rewards playing it safe. A fighter can win by pulling guard and stalling for twelve minutes. They can win by securing a single takedown and defending for the rest of the match. They can win without ever trying to finish — without ever truly putting themselves on the line.

That's not fighting. That's gaming a system.

Submission-only rules strip all of that away. There is nowhere to hide. You must attack. You must take risks. You must commit to the finish or go home with nothing. This format rewards technique, creativity, and genuine grappling ability — not stall tactics and judge manipulation.

It also makes for better competition to watch. Fans understand a rear naked choke. They understand a heel hook, an armbar, a guillotine. There's no scorecard to argue about, no controversial decision. The mat tells the truth every single time.

Submission-only is also the purest link to grappling's origins — Judo, Wrestling, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Catch Wrestling. The goal has always been to control and submit your opponent. We honor that tradition while building something new for Belize.

FOUNDING VISION

“

The vision was never just about Jiu-Jitsu. It was always about what Jiu-Jitsu could open the door to.

We want to bring corporate eyes to this sport. Sponsorships, broadcast deals, brand partnerships — the kind of commercial infrastructure that turns athletes into household names and events into must-watch productions. Our fighters deserve to be compensated not just by us, but by brands that recognise the discipline, grit, and excellence they represent. Monetisation of the athlete is the mission. Prize money is the beginning, not the ceiling.

The five-year plan is bigger than any single event. We're building toward a Central America that is genuinely integrated through sport — where fighters from Belize, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, and beyond compete under one regional banner, and where Belize stands at the center of it.

We want to make Belize City a hub for sport tourism through Jiu-Jitsu. Athletes traveling to compete. Fans traveling to watch. Hotels, gyms, restaurants, and local businesses growing alongside the sport. A sustainable ecosystem where martial arts drives real economic activity and puts Belize on the global map — not just in grappling, but in sports tourism as a whole.

We're building it event by event. But make no mistake — the destination is clear.

— Founder, Finishers Circle
